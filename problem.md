# NextStep (Working Name)

## 🚧 Problem

After leaving structured environments like college, most people struggle with:

- Not knowing what to do next
- Lack of clear direction
- No feedback on progress
- Consuming content without real improvement

Despite learning a lot, they don’t feel job-ready.

---

## 💡 Idea

A system that replaces random learning with structured execution.

Instead of more courses, this helps users:

- Get a clear, personalized path
- Know exactly what to do daily
- Track progress over time
- Identify weak areas
- Move toward real job readiness

---

## 🎯 Vision

Turn an unstructured career journey into a clear, trackable system.

---

## 🧪 Current Status

This project is in the exploration and validation phase.

I’m talking to users, understanding pain points, and refining the idea before building the full product.
